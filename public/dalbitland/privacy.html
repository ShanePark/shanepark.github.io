dalbitLand Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

dalbitLand is a set of offline games for toddlers on iPhone and iPad. This page explains what the app does with information, and what happens when you email the developer for help.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app doesn't collect or send personal information, device identifiers, usage analytics or gameplay data. There are no accounts, sign-in, ads, in-app purchases, tracking or third-party analytics. It never asks for access to the camera, microphone, photos, location, contacts or notifications.

All games, pictures and speech are built into the app. Once installed, it works offline and never connects to a developer server or any outside service.

What stays on your device

The app saves play records and settings on the device: best scores, combos and heights; unlocked and chosen characters; mission and puzzle progress; the car wash collection; and the Korean or English learning language chosen in Word Match. The app never sends these to the developer or anyone else.

Deleting the app deletes these records. Depending on your device and backup settings, app data may be included in an iCloud or computer backup, and restoring that backup can bring the records back. dalbitLand has no accounts and no cloud sync.

If you email for help

Use the inquiry button to open your own email app with the developer’s address. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself. The dalbitLand app never writes or sends email for you.

When you email, the developer receives your email address and whatever you write, and uses it only to reply and sort out the problem. The email passes through the email services used to send and receive it. This is separate from the app, which collects nothing.

Please don't send your child's name, date of birth, photos, voice recordings or other personal details. Instead, tell us the game, your device, its iOS or iPadOS version and what went wrong. If you attach a screenshot, remove any personal information first.

You can use the same inquiry button to ask about, or ask us to delete, anything you sent for support. Deleting the app doesn't delete emails you sent.

Children's privacy

dalbitLand is made for young children. The app collects no personal information from children or adults, and it never asks children to get in touch or share information.

Apple services

App Store downloads and updates, device backups, and any diagnostics or analytics you choose to share with Apple follow Apple's policies and your settings. They are separate from the play records the app keeps on your device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page and its effective date will be updated. For privacy or support questions, use the inquiry button to email the dalbitLand developer.
