Privacy Policy

Lulu’s Little Adventures (루루 어드벤처) · Effective October 28, 2026

In short: the app collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no internet connection. Game progress stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on our side, share or sell any personal information, from children or from anyone else. We never receive your child’s name, photos, voice, location, contacts or device identifiers.

No ads, analytics or tracking

  • No advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics, measurement or crash-reporting services inside the app.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier.
  • No third-party SDKs for ads, analytics, social media or sign-in. The app is built with the open-source Godot Engine, which sends no data.

No internet, no accounts

The app makes no network connections. It has no accounts, logins, chat, in-app purchases or links that leave the app.

Game progress

The game saves its progress automatically in the app’s own storage on your device: where your child is in the world, the chosen character, the play spots they have visited, and the things they have collected, harvested or placed. This file never leaves the device through the app and is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it. If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, Apple’s backup may include it, like other app data.

Microphone, camera and photos

The app never requests or uses the microphone. The game engine includes standard microphone support, so the app bundle contains a microphone usage description, but the app never turns that support on, never records or listens, and never shows a microphone permission prompt. The app does not use the camera or your photo library.

Apple’s optional reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Apple controls this, not the app. You can turn it off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children’s privacy

This app is made for young children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13, in line with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple’s Kids category rules: no ads, no third-party analytics, no purchases and no links out of the app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? The button opens your own email app. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and help. Please don’t include your child’s name, photos or other personal information.

게임 기록

게임은 기기 안의 앱 전용 저장 공간에 자동으로 저장돼요. 아이가 세상 어디에 있는지, 고른 캐릭터, 들러 본 놀이 장소, 모으고 수확하고 놓아 둔 물건이 저장돼요. 이 파일은 앱을 통해 기기 밖으로 나가지 않고 저희에게 전송되지 않아요. 앱을 삭제하면 함께 지워져요. iCloud나 컴퓨터로 기기를 백업하면 다른 앱 데이터처럼 Apple의 백업에 포함될 수 있어요.

마이크, 카메라, 사진

이 앱은 마이크 권한을 요청하거나 마이크를 사용하지 않아요. 게임 엔진에 기본 마이크 기능이 들어 있어서 앱 패키지에 마이크 사용 설명 문구가 포함되어 있지만, 앱은 그 기능을 켜지 않고, 녹음하거나 듣지 않으며, 마이크 권한 창을 띄우지 않아요. 카메라와 사진 보관함도 사용하지 않아요.

Apple의 선택형 보고서

기기 설정에서 앱 개발자와 분석 정보 공유를 켜 두셨다면, Apple이 익명의 오류 보고서와 사용 통계를 저희에게 제공할 수 있어요. 이는 앱이 아니라 Apple이 관리해요. 설정 › 개인정보 보호 및 보안 › 분석 및 향상에서 끌 수 있어요.
